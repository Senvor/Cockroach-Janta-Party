CHAPTER ONE

origin

15 May 2026

The Spark

Remarks made during a Supreme Court hearing by Chief Justice Surya Kant sparked widespread discussion online after the term "cockroaches" became associated with unemployed youth.

16 May 2026

Birth of CJP

Abhijeet Dipke launched the Cockroach Janta Party (CJP), presenting it as a satirical movement and inviting people to join the "voice of the lazy and unemployed."

17–19 May 2026

Rapid Growth

Within days, CJP gained millions of followers online, surpassing established political parties on Instagram and attracting national attention.

May 2026

From Joke to Movement

What began as an internet joke evolved into a platform expressing frustrations about unemployment, examination scandals, and youth issues.

Today

Still Growing

CJP remains one of India's most talked-about digital movements, transforming a controversial remark into a symbol of collective identity.
